# Build with AI: Code for Communities — Second Edition

## What to Build & What to Submit

### Build a Working Prototype, Not Just a Concept

Your submission needs to demonstrate **real, working functionality** and arrive as a complete package that judges can evaluate end to end.

---

## Build

Submissions must demonstrate all of the following:

* **Functioning end-to-end flow** for your track's core use case
* **Mandatory integration of Google AI** — GenAI, predictive modelling, computer vision, or other relevant Google AI capabilities
* **Real or realistic data** — public datasets, sample data, or APIs where live data isn't available
* **Built for India** — the solution should be designed to scale across states and communities, not just a single city
* **Multilingual or voice support** where the track calls for it

---

## Submit

Each team must submit the following:

1. **Source Code**
   A public or access-granted GitHub repository.

2. **Demo Video — 3–5 minutes**
   A working end-to-end walkthrough demonstrating the prototype.

3. **Pitch Deck — 10–12 slides**
   The deck should cover:

   * Problem
   * Solution
   * AI approach
   * Who it serves
   * Why it is deployable
   * How it can scale across India

4. **Brief Description**
   A concise **2–3 line description** of your solution.

5. **Deployed Link**
   A live deployed link to your working prototype.

# Evaluation Parameters

Submissions will be judged across **five criteria**, ranked by weight.

## 25% — AI / Technical Execution

**Is Google AI doing meaningful work? Does the prototype function end-to-end?**

The AI should be a meaningful part of the solution rather than simply being added as a superficial feature.

---

## 20% — Problem-Solution Fit

**Does it directly and specifically address the stated challenge?**

The solution should clearly solve the problem described in the selected track.

---

## 20% — Depth & Reach Across India

**Can this realistically scale from one city or state to communities across India?**

Judges will consider interoperability, scalability, adaptability across regions, and the ability to serve diverse Indian communities.

---

## 20% — Deployability & Scalability

**Could this be piloted within a ministry or across states in weeks?**

The prototype should demonstrate a realistic path from hackathon prototype to real-world deployment.

---

## 15% — Impact Potential

**Scale of benefit — how many people, across how many states, how meaningfully?**

Solutions should demonstrate meaningful potential for social, economic, environmental, or public-service impact at scale.

---

## What Judges Are Looking For

The strongest submissions will go beyond a concept or AI demo. They should demonstrate:

**A real problem → a working AI-powered solution → realistic data → an end-to-end prototype → a credible deployment path → the ability to scale across India.**
